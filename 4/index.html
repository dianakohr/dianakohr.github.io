Model architecture:

My implementation closely followed the project spec, making use of Pytorch for faster operations. My NeRF neural net structure followed the provided diagram, except I used softplus for density instead of ReLU. In terms of training my NeRFs, I used an Adam optimizer with a learning rate of 5e-4. After experimentation, I set my near and far values to 0.25 and 0.5, respectively.
