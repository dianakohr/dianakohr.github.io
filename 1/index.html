Project 1

Some of these photos are fails! The single-scale aligned photos (JPGs only) are here. The complete set of multi-scale pyramid aligned photos are at the bottom.

Single-scale alignment

My first step was to get the skeleton code running and promptly take a 24 hour break. The cathedral on the left is the very first version and has no alignment. I then implemented window search based on the project spec: align(base, other) returns the best offset in the range [-15, 15] from shifting 'other' over 'base'. Since my implementation only compares the overlapping area, I defined my metric as the squared L2 norm (aka Euclidean distance) divided by the number of overlapping pixels. Based on the raw image dimensions, I went with cropping 50 pixels off each edge (to ensure the image contents, not the borders, were being matched). After implementing all this, I did a test run and realized that I completely forgot to track improvements, so I always ended up shifting by [15, 15]. That's where the cathedral on the right comes from :D

After fixing this small issue, single-scale alignment was yielding good results on the JPGs (shown below), but not the TIFs.

Multi-scale pyramid alignment

My first attempt at image pyramid was faulty: being a genius, I took the shifts from the smallest version of the image, and directly applied them to the original image. As you can see in the church on the left, it didn't help... So I turned 'align' into a recursive function that downsamples the image by half until it's at most 500 pixels in height. At that size, it window searches over [-15, 15]. As it returns up the call stack, the offset gets adjusted: 1) it needs to be doubled as the dimensions are doubled, and 2) we search a small window [-1, 1] around it to make use of the higher resolution. The church on the right is the result of this fix.

Doing adjustments at each scale of the image worked for most images, but some were still blurry, and I had to troubleshoot them individually. I expanded the search window to [-30, 30] to fix melons, for example.

I also noticed that I wasn't cropping the edges of the TIF images enough. Increasing the border size from 50 (JPG) to 300 (TIF) worked.

However, emir got blurrier the more I cropped, so emir is the only TIF image that I still crop by 50. Due to certain disparities in the different color channels, emir still isn't aligned perfectly...

And now the multi-scale pyramid aligned results :P
